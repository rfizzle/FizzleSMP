## Removed
- Gear Tinkering — spark profiling traced ~77% of server-thread tick time to its bundled datapack, which runs an unbounded entity scan with NBT predicate every tick. Grind Enchantments covers the core enchantment-extraction workflow without the tick cost.
- Creeper Healing (and Monkey Utils) — removed proactively to reduce server tick budget pressure. The explosion-restore workflow is non-essential.
