Enchanting Infuser now supports Mending and Soulbound, and a startup crash is fixed.

## Fixed
- Server startup crash caused by NeoEnchant's Auto Smelt enchantment having an invalid weight of 0 — corrected to satisfy the 1.21.1 codec range (the enchantment remains effectively disabled)
