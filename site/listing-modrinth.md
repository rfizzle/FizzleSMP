# FizzleSMP — Multiplayer Survival Modpack

**_Forge your path. Master your craft. Shape the world._**

![FizzleSMP logo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/rfizzle/FizzleSMP/master/art/logo.png)

**Also on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/fizzlesmp)
and [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/rfizzle/FizzleSMP/releases).**
Visit the [website](https://smp.rfizzle.com) for the full mod list, install
guide, and server details.

---

FizzleSMP is a handcrafted multiplayer survival experience for **Minecraft
1.21.1 (Fabric)**, built for friends who want more from their world — without
losing what makes Minecraft feel like home.

**Big, but handcrafted — and it runs.** Nearly a hundred biomes, a full RPG
class system, overhauled combat, and community tooling, held together by a
performance stack tuned so it all stays smooth.

## At a glance

- Minecraft **1.21.1** on **Fabric** — managed with [packwiz](https://packwiz.infra.link/).
- **Multiplayer-first**: proximity voice chat, land claims, player shops,
  per-player loot, and waystones.
- Bundles the **Concord** suite (Meridian, Mercantile, Tribulation, Prosperity)
  alongside curated exploration, RPG, magic, tech, and combat mods.
- Tuned to run — a full performance stack (Sodium, Lithium, Noisium, and more);
  **6–8 GB RAM** is plenty.

## Features

### A World Worth Exploring

Every horizon hides something new. Nearly a hundred biomes reshape the Overworld with dramatic cliffs, lush valleys, and forgotten ruins. The Nether burns hotter with Incendium's volcanic wastes, the End stretches into alien landscapes, and hidden dimensions like the Bumblezone and the depths of Deeper and Darker reward the bold. Every dungeon, stronghold, and ocean temple has been rebuilt from the ground up — no two adventures are the same.

### Choose Your Path

There's no single way to play. Train as a Wizard, Paladin, Rogue, Warrior, or Archer through a full RPG class system with spells, skills, and progression trees. Build industrial empires with Tech Reborn and Oritech. Unravel the mysteries of color-based magic with Spectrum. Hunt legendary weapons from massive bosses. Or simply build a farm, open a shop, and watch the economy grow around you.

### Combat That Feels Alive

Forget spam-clicking. FizzleSMP overhauls combat with directional swings, combat rolls, unique weapon types — from claymores to chakrams — and enemies that grow smarter and tougher the longer your world survives. Boss encounters are designed for groups, and the loot is worth the fight.

### Built for Community

Proximity voice chat. Player-run shops and economies. Land claims that protect what you've built. Per-player loot so every chest feels like yours. Waystones to stay connected across a massive world. This is an SMP designed for people who play together.

### Runs Well, Plays Better

Under the hood, a full performance stack (Sodium, Lithium, Noisium, and more) keeps frame rates high and load times low — so the experience stays smooth even with all of this packed in.

## Built on Concord

FizzleSMP is the flagship pack for [Concord](https://github.com/rfizzle/concord)
— a modular collection of Minecraft system overhauls — and ships the whole suite
in one tuned world:

- [Meridian](https://meridian.rfizzle.com) — Chart your enchantments.
- [Mercantile](https://mercantile.rfizzle.com) — Every villager remembers.
- [Tribulation](https://tribulation.rfizzle.com) — Survive what comes next.
- [Prosperity](https://prosperity.rfizzle.com) — Every chest, yours to discover.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.21.1** (Fabric)
- **Java 21** — Temurin recommended; most launchers handle this automatically
- **6–8 GB RAM** allocated — less than 4 GB will crash with this many mods
- A launcher that imports `.mrpack` or CurseForge packs — Modrinth App, Prism
  Launcher, MultiMC, or the CurseForge App

## Installation

The **Modrinth App** installs FizzleSMP in a couple of clicks — it fetches the
mods and sets the Java version for you.

1. Open the [Modrinth App](https://modrinth.com/app), search for **FizzleSMP**,
   and install it (or install directly from this page).
2. Set the instance memory to **6–8 GB** if it isn't already.
3. Launch the pack and connect to the server.

Prefer another launcher? Download the latest `.mrpack` or `.zip` from
[GitHub Releases](https://github.com/rfizzle/FizzleSMP/releases) and import it
into Prism Launcher, MultiMC, or the CurseForge App. Full walkthrough:
[smp.rfizzle.com/install.html](https://smp.rfizzle.com/install.html)
